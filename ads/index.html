Who's Nife?

Hi, I'm Nife (pronounced Knife). While Nife isn't my real name, it's the name I go by online. I'm a thumbnail designer and video editor for YouTubers and gaming creators.

I'm passionate about graphic design and creating thumbnails and edits that help videos, and creators, stand out. I work mainly with Minecraft and gaming creators, putting together thumbnails, shorts, and full video edits that are built to get clicks without looking generic.

If you'd like to see examples of my work, check out my thumbnails and edits, or head to the pricing page if you're interested in working together.
